# AI Portfolio Research Dashboard

An AI-powered investment research platform that enables investors to analyze, compare, forecast, and optimize stock portfolios through interactive visualizations, predictive analytics, and AI-generated investment insights.

---

## Overview

The AI Portfolio Research Dashboard is an end-to-end financial analytics application designed to support investment research and portfolio decision-making.

The platform combines real-time market data, machine learning-based forecasting, portfolio performance analytics, and AI-assisted research into a single interactive dashboard.

---

## Business Problem

Retail investors often rely on multiple platforms to perform portfolio analysis, stock comparison, forecasting, and investment research.

This project consolidates these workflows into a unified application, enabling users to:

- Monitor portfolio performance
- Compare multiple securities
- Forecast future prices
- Generate AI-assisted investment insights
- Visualize portfolio allocation and risk
